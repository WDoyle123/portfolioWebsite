About Me

I'm a Cloud / DevOps Engineer at FutureOn with a strong passion for building software that works reliably in the real world, not just writing code, but deploying it, scaling it, automating it, and owning the full lifecycle from idea to production.

My day-to-day work spans Kubernetes, Terraform, and multi-cloud infrastructure across GCP, AWS, and Azure, where I design deployment pipelines, engineer internal tooling, and improve the reliability of production systems. I hold both the Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA) and HashiCorp Terraform Associate certifications. With a Masters of Physics degree from the University of Portsmouth, I bring a rigorous analytical approach to solving complex engineering problems.

Beyond my professional role, I actively build and operate personal web applications, automation tooling, and self-hosted services on a bare-metal Kubernetes homelab running on my own hardware. I enjoy owning the full stack from: application code to networking, storage, deployment, monitoring, and long-term maintenance, rather than outsourcing understanding to third-party platforms.

That combination of software engineering, infrastructure ownership, and system-level curiosity is what defines me most as an engineer: I do not just want systems to function — I want to understand exactly how and why they function.
